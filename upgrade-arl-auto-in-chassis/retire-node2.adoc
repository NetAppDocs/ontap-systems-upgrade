= Retire node2
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
To retire node2, you shut down node2 correctly and then remove it from the rack or chassis.

.Steps

. Resume the operation:
+
`system controller replace resume`
+
The node halts automatically.


.What's next?
After retiring node2, choose one of the following controller replacement procedures based on your upgrade configuration:

* link:replace-node2-affa250-affc250.html[Replace the node2 AFF A250 or AFF C250 controller module]
* link:replace-node2-affa800-affc800.html[Replace the node2 AFF A800 or AFF C800 controller module]
* link:replace-node2-affa220-affa200-affc190-fas2620-fas2720.html[Replace the node2 AFF A220, AFF A200, AFF C190, FAS2620, or FAS2720 controller module]
* link:replace-node2-aff700-fas900.html[Replace the node2 AFF A700 or FAS9000 controller and NVRAM modules]
