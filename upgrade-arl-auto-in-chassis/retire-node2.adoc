= Retire node2
:hardbreaks:
:nofooter:
:icons: font
:linkattrs:
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
To retire node2, you shut down node2 correctly and then remove it from the rack or chassis.

.Steps

. Resume the operation:
+
`system controller replace resume`
+
The node halts automatically.


.What's next?
After retiring node2, choose one of the following install procedures based on your upgrade configuration:

* link:replace-node2-affa250-affc250.html[Replace the node2 AFF A250 or AFF C250 controller module]
* link:install-affa90-affa70-on-node2.html[Replace the node1 AFF A800 or AFF C800 controller module]
* link:install-asa150-affa150-fas2820-on-node2.html[Replace the AFF A220, AFF A200, AFF C190, FAS2620, or FAS2720 controller module]
* link:install-asa900-affa900-fas9500-on-node2.html[Replace the AFF A700 or FAS9000 controller and NVRAM modules]
